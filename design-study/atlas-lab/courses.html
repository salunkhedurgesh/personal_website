Featured course / EPFL

Kinematics grounded
motion planning for robots.

ENG-654 connects analytical understanding with practical robot models, interactive lectures and simulation exercises.

How many configurations reach a target? What changes near a singularity? Why can a sequence of individually reachable points still make a difficult path?

The course develops these questions through forward and inverse kinematics, singularity analysis and planning with constraints. A final project brings the pieces together into a kinematics-grounded planning pipeline.
