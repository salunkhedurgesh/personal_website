Current investigations

Four connected directions.

Research explains the agenda and established results. Here, follow the particular questions and people behind the work.

01 / Learning & geometry

How can robot behavior become more explainable?

Kinematic intelligence: augmenting robot learning with analytical properties of the robot, so its geometry informs the learned behavior.

With Sthithpragya Gupta · EPFL, Lausanne

Related result · Science Robotics 2026

02 / Geometric analysis

Can geometry make inverse kinematics easier to understand?

Use Conformal Geometric Algebra to study robot kinematics through geometric objects and their relationships, building intuition alongside analytical models.

With Abhilash Nayak · CSIC, Barcelona

Related work · Generic 3R inverse kinematics · PDF

03 / Mechanisms & actuation

What might the next generation of robot joints look like?

Investigate novel kinematic actuation and robot architectures. The spherical X-joint study is one entry into the relationship between geometry, mobility and remote actuation.

With Vimalesh Muralidharan · IIT Bhubaneswar

Related work · The spherical X-joint · PDF

04 / Transfer learning

Can a robot learn from simpler building blocks?

Investigate transfer learning with sub-domain guarantees: connecting the learning problem to smaller domains in which properties can be understood.

With Bernardo Fichera · Max Planck, Tübingen
