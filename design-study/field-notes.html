My research connects mathematical understanding with the physical structure of a robot. I’m interested in how that connection can inform both the way we move robots and the way we build them.

Why this direction

A researcher’s reading room

Warm paper, forest green, and generous margins suggest a working notebook. Georgia gives questions an editorial voice; system sans keeps descriptions, controls, and citations clear. Body text is 18px, with longer passages kept to readable line lengths.

Make the science approachable

Question-led themes connect projects and publications. Short explanations precede formal titles. Students get a visible learning route; peers get direct access to evidence. Career details belong in About and a future CV link.

Clarity before spectacle

Borrow Apple’s hierarchy, spacing, and control consistency. Keep the diagram specific to robotics. Filters have visible selection states; links work without hover. No autoplay, no decorative toggles. Future playgrounds can grow from these research questions.
