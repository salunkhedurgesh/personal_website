01 / Research

The questions behind
the motion.

A robot’s reachable positions are only part of the story. The connections between its configurations determine what it can actually do.

Q.01

How many ways can a robot reach the same pose?

Different arrangements of the joints can put the tool in exactly the same place. Understanding these solutions exposes choices that a single numerical answer can hide.

Inverse kinematics · Redundancy
Q.02

Can a robot change posture without crossing a singularity?

Some robot geometries allow surprising connections between solutions. Cuspidality helps explain when a robot can change posture while maintaining nonsingular motion.

Cuspidality · Global geometry
Q.03

Why can a reachable path still be impossible to follow?

Reaching each point separately does not guarantee one continuous motion. The starting configuration, joint limits, and connections between solutions all matter.

Why this direction?

The idea: a quiet research instrument. Graphite surfaces give real robot figures room to stand out; cyan identifies links, and the content begins with questions a student can understand. Clear text labels carry the meaning.

The tradeoff: strong continuity with future visual experiments, but long bibliographies and academic reading may feel more comfortable on a light surface. This is a visual concept; the preview is a still, and links lead to existing resources.
