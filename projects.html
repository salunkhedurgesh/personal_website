European Network of Excellence 2024 – Present EPFL LASA

EuRobin · European Robotics & AI Network of Excellence

Context: EuRobin unites European academic and industrial leaders to advance transfer learning, embodied AI, and cognitive robotic systems capable of operating in real-world human environments.

Scientific Contribution: Leading investigations on Kinematic Intelligence: enabling robots to transfer demonstrated skills without breaking when deployed across different robot morphologies, link lengths, or joint boundaries. By embedding analytical singularity loci and joint limits directly into dynamical systems, skills transfer safely across both redundant and non-redundant serial robots.

Collaborators: Sthithpragya Gupta, Prof. Aude Billard (EPFL LASA)

EU Horizon Europe 2023 – 2025 EPFL & Consortia

DARKO · Dynamic Agile Resilient Kinematic Operations

Context: European Horizon initiative targeting agile intralogistics robots, dual-arm mobile manipulators, and collaborative operations in changing warehouse environments.

Scientific Contribution: Investigating the kinematic behavior of kinematically redundant collaborative manipulators (7-DOF arms like the Franka Emika Panda and KUKA LBR iiwa). Discovered and proved that design offsets in commercial cobots induce cuspidality, allowing robots to unexpectedly change inverse kinematic postures without passing through singularities. Formulated the first mathematical reduction of 7R chains to parameterized 3R equivalents.

Collaborators: Sthithpragya Gupta, Prof. Aude Billard (EPFL LASA)

ANR France · FWF Austria 2020 – 2023 LS2N & Sorbonne

ECARP · Efficient & Certified Algorithms for Robot Motion Planning

Context: High-level bilateral research initiative uniting computer algebra, real algebraic geometry, and robotics between LS2N (Nantes), Sorbonne Université (Paris), JKU (Linz), and Univ. Innsbruck.

Scientific Contribution: Comprehensive analysis of cuspidal commercial serial manipulators (including Kinova JACO and FANUC CRX). Disproved decade-old assumptions that cuspidality is restricted to academic oddities, demonstrating that neglected cuspidal properties in industrial cobots cause catastrophic path failures and non-repeatable motion planning.

Team: Philippe Wenger, Damien Chablat, Mohab Safey El Din, Andreas Mueller, Tobias Marauli, Jose Capco

FAME · Clinical Research 2020 – 2022 Centrale Nantes & CHU

FAME · Parallel Manipulators for Otological Microsurgery

Context: Joint surgical robotics collaboration between École Centrale de Nantes, Centre Hospitalier Universitaire (CHU) Nantes, DFKI Germany, and the University of Genoa.

Scientific Contribution: Design optimization and kinematic synthesis of parallel kinematic mechanisms (PKM) providing Remote Center of Motion (RCM) constraints for endoscope handling during minimally invasive ear and sinus surgeries. Combined Nelder-Mead local search with low-discrepancy global sampling to optimize workspace dexterity under strict anatomical patient constraints.

Collaborators: Damien Chablat, Guillaume Michel, Philippe Bordure, Shivesh Kumar, Marcello Sanguineti

Robotics & CIM 2017 – 2019 IIT Jodhpur & DFKI

Sensorless Full-Body Active Compliance on 6-RSS Parallel Platform

Context: Physical human-robot collaboration on high-payload closed-loop parallel mechanisms without relying on fragile multi-axis force/torque load cells.

Scientific Contribution: Formulated and experimentally validated a three-layer cascaded impedance controller using actuator motor current estimation to deliver full-body reactive compliance against human contact at any point on the platform.

Collaborators: Anirvan Dutta, Suril V. Shah, Arun Dayal Udai, Shivesh Kumar

Theoretical Kinematics 2024 – Present CSIC & IIT

Conformal Geometric Algebra & Rolling Kinematics

Context: International theoretical exploration into coordinate-free kinematics and bio-inspired mechanism design with CSIC (Barcelona) and IIT Bhubaneswar.

Scientific Contribution: Applying Conformal Geometric Algebra (CGA) to reformulate inverse kinematic models as intersections of geometric circles, yielding univariate polynomials without painful joint elimination. Designing the Spherical X-Joint: a 4R mechanism demonstrating pure rolling motion between elliptical cones for lightweight bio-inspired robotic wrists.
