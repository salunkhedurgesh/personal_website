# Durgesh Salunkhe — Robotics Studio

A static, editable personal site. No package install, build command, database, server-side scripts or API keys. For local development, serve this directory with `python3 -m http.server 8000` and open `http://localhost:8000`. To deploy, upload the contents alongside your existing site's resources. `index.html` is the entry point. Preserve `projects/main/resources/` and the original course, paper and project paths; PDF, BibTeX and supporting-page links refer to that archive, not duplicate files in this package. Preview / test on a separate branch or subdirectory first. Nothing was published automatically.

## Letter atelier — interaction and rendering

The homepage still uses Live calendar; `?edition=holi` opens Holi and `?edition=puppets` opens Marionette. Five immediately accessible study buttons are under the name. The standalone review HTML opens in Holi. C/E/J still selects the clay form. Drag an individual glyph upward and release it, or tab to the name, choose a letter with the arrow keys, and hold/release Space. Drag the empty stage horizontally to see a small change of viewing angle. The art is rendered in Three.js, not a flattened image of the concept sheet. The powder combines depth-layered textured surfaces with instanced 3D particles. Its movement is artistic, not a fluid simulation.

- `js/clay/atelier.js`, `carving.js`, `images/atelier/`: five new sets and material studies. Hollow jack-o’-lantern shell with cutouts; flag fabric and fabric movement; original India Gate and Eiffel Tower miniatures; carved oak rail, eyelets, cords and stage boards. The three variants share the theme textures but retain their individual clay meshes. No third-party seasonal prop packs.
- `js/clay/play.js`: ray picking, accessible keyboard controls, bounded lift, ordinary gravity / rebound and a damped, distance-attenuated support impulse. A letter's first landing sends a delayed pulse to the others; it is not a camera shake. This is intended to be playful, not an engineering model of contact transmission.
- `js/clay/jolt-rig.js`: on-demand JoltPhysics.js 1.1.0 with one dynamic box proxy per glyph, a fixed beam and floor, an individual distance constraint per string, fixed time steps, and a handheld velocity controller. Only opening Marionette downloads Jolt. If the module cannot be loaded, the scene switches to a small local spring simulation, explicitly labelled **Offline motion**. The module is not bundled by default. Source: https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics.js (MIT), npm package `jolt-physics@1.1.0`.
- `tools/jolt-check.html`: run this served page on your deployment to exercise the actual WASM module, create fifteen dynamic bodies and distance constraints, advance 600 steps, check the transfer to the world and release it. Do not interpret a working local animation as a positive Jolt test.
- Publishing with no dependency on a CDN: `python3 tools/vendor_jolt.py` downloads the official package from the npm registry, validates its SHA-512 and saves the module and MIT notice locally before enabling local loading. It is an optional, once-only publishing utility, not a website build or backend. The same script takes `--standalone /path/to/durgesh-letter-atelier.html` to make a separate self-contained copy including Jolt. For a public site with a tight CSP allow WebAssembly compilation; do not remove site-wide security restrictions just for this feature. The ordinary lift/drop scene does not need Jolt or a network connection.
- Source for generating the paint, textures and pumpkin is under `tools/art/`. These tools are NOT dependencies of the site. The separate GLB collection contains editable geometry and PBR materials in still poses, not the JS interaction layer.

## Pages and editing

- `#home`, `#publications`, `#projects`, `#teaching` are static hash routes. The home role can be linked directly with `#home?role=cnrs`, `kin`, `chess` or `teach` (for example `#home?role=kin`). Projects and publications support `?theme=...`; `#teaching?course=nantes` opens the Nantes record.
- `js/catalog.js`: biography, publications, projects, gallery captions and player credits. The biography preserves the author's source wording, including the description of the EPFL postdoc. Review the tense when the CNRS appointment starts. Project tags index the supplied archive; no PI/co-PI role is inferred from a funder.
- `js/site.js`: courses, teaching philosophy (draft copy), searchable directories and exhibit navigation. Projects and Teaching follow the author's Ideation sketches, pp. 11–12. The six project descriptions/galleries and four original bibliography tables come from the uploaded website. There are 25 publication, chapter, thesis and workshop records, plus 6 talks listed separately by type. Select the type filter to distinguish them. Some published versions were updated against their publisher or institutional record. The author’s Scholar profile is linked, not scraped or kept in live sync.
- `js/scene.js`: calendar routing for the clay studio, four 3D navigation icons, parallel/serial 3D exhibits and calendar. Models are loaded when required. The two mechanism exhibits and header share a renderer; the office scene and IK view retain their own rendering code. Devices without WebGL receive prerendered clay lettering, a lightweight assembly animation, rendered navigation icons and accessible model previews. Reduced motion is respected. Models are visual exhibits, not collision-certified simulations.
- `projects/main/tour/`: CNRS mark, office, Strasbourg, planet, 6-slider GoFa FK/IK playground. Opening the robot preserves the launching context. `src/bridge.js` connects the room, map and standalone IK view. A fixed z=0.50 m XY slice uses Rz(0) Ry(−0.28) Rx(1.47); full view and independently sampled central detail each use 400 × 250 poses. Click a point, or use the arrows and Enter, to solve for that target; the map is not a path-validity certificate. Bundled before-/after-limits images were independently regenerated using the supplied native solver with a polynomial fallback for unresolved samples. When available, the original course JSON replaces the reference maps. The standalone/native rendering of the static site does not execute a server-side binary. Sources: https://epfl-lasa.github.io/eng-654/lectures_main/lectures/lecture_07.html#slide-22 and https://github.com/epfl-lasa/eng-654/tree/main/lectures_main/assets/data/lecture07 .
- `images/chalk.svg`: chalk rendition of lecture 07 slide 18, using the full native workspace and joint-space curves and the default closed workspace path. `images/fig-kin.webp`, `fig-design.webp`, `fig-transfer.webp`: image crops of IJRR Fig. 1 (10.1177/02783649241293481), MMT Fig. 5 (10.1016/j.mechmachtheory.2022.104796), and Science Robotics Fig. 1A (10.1126/scirobotics.aea1995). Review permissions for the publisher images and the stylised CNRS mark before public release. The published existing models, logos and article assets retain their own rights.

## Chess

The board is an independent standard-chess study interface, not an embedded Lichess client. Twenty historical games open at characteristic positions. Guess or reveal the players to read notes; flip the board, navigate its main line and variations, and draw coloured arrows/circles. Right-drag draws and right-click circles; Shift red, Alt blue, Alt+Shift gold, plain green. Select Draw for touch. The optional analysis switch enables legal variations and evaluation. PGN import supports standard chess, setup FENs, multiple games, nested variations, brace comments, numeric annotation glyphs and common `%cal`/`%csl` drawings. Save PGN preserves the variation tree and current drawings. Private PGNs are parsed locally; evaluation sends the displayed FEN, not the PGN, to https://lichess.org/api/cloud-eval . Cloud results display the source and depth. The Lichess API returns only cached scores; uncached/offline positions use a short local, non-Stockfish search, marked “Local estimate.” It is useful for a quick reference, not a replacement for deep engine analysis. Player portrait licenses/attribution are on the cards; images use Wikimedia Commons. No account, challenge impersonation, multiplayer back end or dependency on Lichess code is included.

## Calendar lettering and weather

Live calendar is based on Europe/Paris dates. The menu beside the role selector previews all editions independently of the real date or weather. Fixed festivals have precedence over weather, then snowfall, summer heat (>35 °C, June–August), then occasional robot-carrier and marionette days. March 14 adds a π inscription. Strasbourg weather uses https://open-meteo.com/ (Weather data by Open-Meteo, CC BY 4.0); cached for up to 15 minutes. If the service is unreachable and no fresh reading is available, the header uses calendar-only effects, never fabricated weather. Holidays and 3D exhibits work offline. Movable Holi/Diwali dates are explicitly tabled in `js/scene.js` through 2031; update them as official calendars are published. For 2027 the Holi entry follows the Government of India's published overseas holiday list (March 23). Future dates are provisional. References: https://www.hcilondon.gov.in/page/list-of-the-holidays/ and https://www.timeanddate.com/holidays/india/ .

## Clay lettering

Use the C / E / J controls to select and remember a finish. Compare shows all three single-line forms. The lettering always uses one baseline, including on phones. The role selector and academic content are unchanged. Use the calendar selector to choose **Robot assembly** and the on-stage Replay control to run it again. The left-hand robot is the original articulated ABB GoFa, operating on D. The other is an original four-axis SCARA, operating on the S immediately before the H in DURGESH. The S of SALUNKHE stays mounted. The grippers first align and lower the two letters, then open and retreat. The motion is a visual study, not a hardware program.

Files for editing: `js/clay/studio.js` (camera, studio lights, materials, controls), `forms.js` (compact indexed letter and snow geometry), `props.js` (seasonal props and movement), `arms.js` and `motion.js` (assembly rigs and GoFa joint samples), `cathedral.js` (a scaled copy of the city's original cathedral), and `images/clay-*.webp` (surface grain and fallback renders). The supplied local GoFa GLB supplies the articulated arm geometry. The marionette edition can load the pinned Jolt WASM runtime; no build is necessary. The assembly stops at the end; decorative animations sleep offscreen. When reduced motion is preferred, the finished scene is shown. For direct links use `?clay=E&edition=robots` or `?clay=J&edition=standard`. Add `&lettering` for a lettering-only view.
