About me

I am a CNRS Chargé de Recherche in the RDH Team at ICube, Strasbourg. I work on kinematics, control and design of robots that share space with people — from everyday collaboration to the operating room. Current threads: kinematic intelligence for safe, explainable learning and cross-robot skill transfer; Conformal Geometric Algebra for kinematic analysis; and mechanically intelligent designs such as tensegrity-inspired joints and rolling-kinematics platforms.

Previously a postdoc at LASA, EPFL. PhD at LS2N, Nantes, on cuspidal robots with Prof. Philippe Wenger and Prof. Damien Chablat. My work appears in Science Robotics, IJRR, IEEE RA-L and Mechanism and Machine Theory, and at ICRA and IROS.

I teach Kinematics-grounded robot motion planning at IEEE RAS University, and have given invited talks at EPFL, IISc Bengaluru, LAAS-CNRS and the SIMERO summer school. I serve as Associate Editor for ICRA 2027 and review for IJRR, RA-L, ICRA and IROS.
I welcome enquiries for PhD and internship positions.

In research, I believe: “Learning alone is blind; analysis alone is brittle. Robots should be intelligent by design and adaptive by experience.”
