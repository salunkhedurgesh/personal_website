Geographic imagery and data in this edition
Earth imagery: NASA Blue Marble via Matplotlib Basemap's bmng.jpg.
Regional relief: shadedrelief.com and NASA Blue Marble via Basemap.
Coasts / political boundaries: GSHHG 2.3.6 / GMT as distributed with basemap-data 2.0.0.
The atlas adds its own color treatment, outline of metropolitan France, legends, geographic markers and perspective projection. This is not imagery from Google Maps or Google Earth.
Original distribution, including the input rasters and vector data: https://pypi.org/project/basemap-data/2.0.0/ . Build script: tools/planet_textures.py in the source edition. The regional map and data-derived outline may be regenerated from this published distribution. The data bundle assigns LGPL-3.0-or-later to the boundary data and MIT to the other files, subject to the standard exceptions described in its package notice. Copies of the notices follow in this directory.
See https://matplotlib.org/basemap/stable/users/geography.html and https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/blue-marble-next-generation/ for context and attribution.
