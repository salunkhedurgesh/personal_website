Path correspondence

The blue path and the red executed path are deliberately independent. The joint trajectory is animated according to its own row index. If both files contain different numbers of samples, no implicit resampling is performed.

The workspace CSV defines the desired Cartesian path. Its position columns generate the blue curve, while its quaternion columns define the discrete RGB end-effector frames. The joint CSV is executed through the URDF kinematic chain, and the resulting FK end-effector positions accumulate as the red trace.
